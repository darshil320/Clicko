# ADR-0002 — Clickco is principal / seller of record, not a marketplace

**Status:** Accepted · **Source:** v0.2 §1, §11, §12

Clickco invoices the client for the full booking value. 18% GST (SAC 998383) is Clickco's output liability on the whole amount. Photographers are independent contractors paid per job against a (self-billed) vendor invoice, with TDS under 194C/194J.

**Consequences:** contractor-status hygiene is a product requirement (no exclusivity, declining allowed, per-job pay, deliverable not method); GST-registered vendors preferred for ITC; TDS automated from day one.
