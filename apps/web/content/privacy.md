# Privacy Policy

{{BRAND}} is operated by {{ENTITY}} ("we"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

## What we collect

- **Account:** your name and mobile number, verified by one-time password.
- **Bookings:** venue address and pincode, shoot date and time, the contact details of anyone you book for, and any special requirements you share.
- **Payments:** processed by our payment gateway. We never see or store your full card or UPI credentials.
- **Location:** for photographers only, device location at check-in and check-out to confirm attendance and billing.
- **Shoot files:** the photographs taken at your booking.

## Why we use it

To take and confirm bookings, assign a photographer, process payment, deliver your files, send service messages (WhatsApp, SMS, push), resolve complaints, and meet our tax and legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

Your assigned photographer receives only what they need to do the shoot; calls between you are masked so neither side sees the other's real number. We also share data with service providers who run parts of the service for us: payment processing, cloud file storage, messaging, mapping and analytics. We do not sell personal data.

## Your photographs

Files are delivered through links issued by us. RAW files are deleted 7 days after delivery and JPEG copies 30 days after delivery. We use your photos in our own marketing **only if you opt in** when booking, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

## Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or delete your shoot files before the retention period ends. Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Complaints can also be raised with our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/).

## Retention

We keep account and booking records for as long as your account is active and as required by tax law. Shoot files follow the windows above.

## Changes

We will notify you in the app before any material change to this policy.
