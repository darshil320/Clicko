# Privacy Policy

{{BRAND}} is operated by {{ENTITY}}, {{ADDRESS}} ("we"). We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025. This notice says what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how to use your rights.

## Who can use Clickco

Clickco accounts are for people aged 18 or over. You confirm this when you sign in. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data, except what the law requires us to keep.

## What we collect

**If you book a shoot**
- **Account:** your mobile number (verified by one-time password), and your name and email if you add them.
- **Bookings:** venue address and pincode, shoot date and time, the name and number of anyone you book for, and any special requirements you share.
- **Payments:** the payment method and the UPI ID or last 4 digits of your card, from our payment gateway. Full card numbers, UPI PINs and bank logins go to the gateway only. We never see or store them.
- **Support:** messages you send us, photo-problem reports, refund requests and reviews.
- **Shoot files:** the photographs and video taken at your booking. They may show other people at your event.

**If you are a photographer**
- **Profile:** name, mobile number, city, equipment, availability and work samples.
- **Verification documents:** PAN card, a photo ID (driving licence, passport or voter ID), address proof, police verification, bank proof, GST certificate if you have one, drone licences if you fly, equipment invoices and your signed contract. We never ask for or store an Aadhaar number.
- **Tax and payout details:** PAN, GSTIN, and bank account or UPI ID for payouts. Bank account numbers are stored encrypted.
- **Location:** your device's location when you check in, check out or report an on-site event (such as waiting time), to confirm attendance and billing. We do not track you in the background.
- **Device:** a random install ID used to sign your device in.

**From everyone**
- **Technical:** the IP address of each one-time-password request, kept to stop abuse.
- **Waitlist:** your name, number and city if you join a waitlist on our website.
- **Calls:** calls between a client and a photographer go through a masked number. We keep the call time and length, never a recording.

## Why we use it

To take and confirm bookings; assign a photographer; process payments, refunds and payouts; deliver your files; send service messages by WhatsApp (only if you opt in), SMS and push; verify photographers and keep clients safe; resolve complaints; and meet our tax and legal obligations. We use each item only for the purpose it was collected for.

## What needs your consent

- **Marketing use of your photos.** We feature photos from your shoot in our marketing **only if you opt in** at booking. It is off unless you turn it on. We never feature a photo in which a child can be identified, even if you opt in.
- **WhatsApp messages.** Sent only if you opt in. Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} to stop them.
- **Marketing messages.** We do not send any today. If we start, we will ask first.

You can withdraw any consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: in the app where it exists, or by writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before, and it does not stop the service you booked.

## Who receives your data

- **Your photographer or client:** only what is needed for the shoot. Neither side sees the other's real number.
- **Service providers who process data for us:** Razorpay (payments), Google (file storage on Google Drive), Meta (WhatsApp messages), MSG91 (SMS), Exotel (masked calls), Expo (push notifications), Railway (our servers) and Vercel (our website).
- **Authorities:** when the law requires it, such as tax authorities or a valid legal order.

Some of these providers process data outside India, including in Singapore and the United States. We only use countries the Government of India has not restricted. We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

- **RAW files:** deleted 7 days after delivery.
- **JPEG copies:** deleted 30 days after delivery.
- **Photographer verification documents:** deleted 180 days after the relationship ends or an application is closed.
- **Account and booking records:** kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your name, number and email and keep only the invoice and payment records GST law requires, for 72 months from the annual return's due date.
- **One-time-password records and IP addresses:** kept only as long as needed to prevent abuse.
- **System logs:** kept for one year, as the DPDP Rules require.

## Your rights

You can:
- **Access** a summary of your personal data and who we shared it with.
- **Correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data. Clients can delete their account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or through our [account deletion page](/delete-account/). Photographers can ask through support.
- **Withdraw consent**, as described above.
- **Nominate** someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
- **Complain** to our [Grievance Officer](/grievance/). We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.

Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} for any of these. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Security and breaches

Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it, logged, and protected in transit and at rest. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay. We will say what happened, what it may mean for you, and what we are doing about it.

## Changes

We will tell you in the app before any material change to this notice.
