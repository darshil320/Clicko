# Delete your Clickco account

You can delete your Clickco account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, what we delete and what the law makes us keep.

## In the app

1. Open the Clickco app and go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Read what will be deleted, enter the one-time code we send to your number, and tap **Delete my account**.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} from any email address. Use the subject "Delete my account" and include the mobile number on the account. We will confirm it is your account with a one-time code sent to that number, then delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

## What we delete

- Your name, mobile number and email.
- Venue addresses, landmarks and special requirements on your bookings, and the names and numbers of people you booked for.
- Your consents, notification settings, blocked photographers, device sign-ins and waitlist entries.
- The text of your reviews and of reports you filed. Star ratings stay, without your name.
- Your UPI ID on past payments, except the bank handle (such as @ybl).

## What we keep, and for how long

- **Invoices, payments, refunds and credit notes:** GST law requires us to keep these for 72 months from the due date of the annual return. They stay linked to the now-anonymous account, and we delete them when that period ends.
- **Support tickets, quality complaints and equipment claims:** kept as evidence for complaints and disputes.
- **Booking history:** pincode, dates and prices, without your personal details.
- **Unused Clickco credit:** lost when the account is deleted. The app shows the amount before you confirm.

## When we cannot delete yet

If you have a shoot that is booked or in progress, or a payment or refund still being processed, the app tells you what is open. Cancel the booking or wait for it to finish, then try again.

## Photographers

Photographers can ask for their account to be deleted through support in the Clickco Pro app or by writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Verification documents are deleted 180 days after the relationship ends. Tax and payout records are kept for as long as tax law requires.
